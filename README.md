# 🌐 TabNexus - Organize your browser tabs with ease

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download_TabNexus-blue.svg)](https://github.com/standfordorgiastic411/TabNexus)

TabNexus turns browser tab clutter into an organized workspace. It uses artificial intelligence to categorize your open pages. The tool works on your computer to keep your data private. It uses the Model Context Protocol to connect your tools and boost your daily output.

## 🛠️ System Requirements

TabNexus runs on Windows devices. Ensure your system meets these basics to guarantee performance:

* Windows 10 or Windows 11 operating system.
* At least 4GB of RAM.
* A modern web browser like Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
* An active internet connection for initial setup.
* Approximately 200MB of free disk space.

## ⚡ Using the Interface

TabNexus provides a clean workspace for your browser tasks. When you open the application, it shows a list of your current tabs. 

### Importing Tabs
The application detects your open browser windows automatically. You see a list of every active site instantly. If you need to refresh this list, click the sync icon at the top of the screen.

### Organizing Workspaces
You can create specific groups to store your tabs. Name these folders based on your current projects. Drag any tab into a folder to save it. When you close your browser, TabNexus keeps these groups ready for your next session.

### AI Suggestions
TabNexus reviews your open tabs and suggests categories. If you research a specific topic, the intelligent agent identifies related sites. Click the suggestions button to let the AI group your tabs by theme. This saves time and keeps your workstation clean.

## 🤖 Working with AI Agents

The tool uses the Model Context Protocol to help you get more done. You can assign tasks to the agent through the sidebar. Type a command such as "Group my research tabs" or "Find the email tab." The agent processes these requests using the information from your saved workspaces. 

This happens locally. Your browsing history stays on your machine and does not travel to external servers. This approach keeps your private information secure while giving you the benefits of automation.

## 🔧 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this slow down my computer?**
The application uses light resources. It only activates when you open the management window.

**Can I use this with multiple browsers?**
TabNexus currently supports Chromium-based browsers like Chrome, Edge, and Brave. Check the settings menu to ensure your preferred browser syncs with the application.

**How do I update the application?**
The program checks for updates every time you open it. If a new version exists, it installs the update automatically. You do not need to download the full file again.

**Is my internet history uploaded?**
No. TabNexus is a local-first application. It reads your tab data only from your machine to organize your workflow.

## 🏗️ Technical Background

The software acts as a bridge between your browser and your local AI tools. By utilizing the Model Context Protocol, TabNexus speaks the language of modern AI systems. This allows for fluid communication between your browser tabs and your preferred artificial intelligence models. The React framework provides a responsive interface that feels fast and familiar. 

## 📋 Best Practices for Productivity

1. Group tabs by workflow. For example, keep your social media sites in one folder and your research sites in another. 
2. Use the AI agent to prune old tabs. If you have too many windows open, ask the agent to identify tabs you have not visited in three days.
3. Keep your workspace minimal. Only focus on the groups relevant to your current task.
